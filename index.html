Overview

A student version of the story

This page is inspired by the structure of the Anne Frank House website, but the writing, layout, and visuals are simplified for a school project.

01

Who was Anne?

Anne Frank was born in Frankfurt am Main in 1929. Her family later moved to Amsterdam as antisemitism grew in Nazi Germany.

02

Why the diary matters

Anne received a diary for her thirteenth birthday. While hiding, she wrote about daily life, fear, hope, and her dream of becoming a writer.

03

What students can discuss

Her story helps students explore prejudice, the impact of war on young people, and the responsibility to speak against hate.

Secret Annex

Life in hiding was quiet, crowded, and dangerous

Eight people eventually lived in the Secret Annex. During the day they had to be very careful because workers were in the building below.

The group had little privacy, limited movement, and constant fear of discovery. Anne used writing to process what she saw and felt.

Otto Frank's trusted helpers brought food, news, and support while taking great personal risks.
